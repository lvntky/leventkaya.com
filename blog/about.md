Hi, I'm Levent. I'm a Computer Scientist from Istanbul, Turkey.

## I Make Programs

My relationship with programming is quite complex. I earn money doing this professionally, but I also believe that coding is a way for people to express themselves. In that context, for me, making programs are not only a way to earn money, but also a wonderful combination of craftsmanship, artistry, engineering, science and of cource **understanding**.

## Projects

I'm a huge fan of OSS. I usually spend my free time working on it. Sometimes this involves writing Proof-of-Concept (PoC) programs to help me understand a concept, and other times it consists of libraries or dev-tools I write to improve and contribute to the experience of other programmers. I generally publish blog posts or share short technical notes on this site about the things I program. Here are a few projects that I think are good:

- **[fbgl](https://github.com/lvntky/fbgl)** — A zero-dependency Linux framebuffer graphics library written in C. Direct `/dev/fb0` access, no SDL, no OpenGL.
- **[ArtilleryOS](https://github.com/lvntky/ArtilleryOS)** - Ggraphical operating system that combines the simplicity and power of Unix-like systems. Featuring a custom-designed kernel. *Currently Archived/Abandoned*
- **[cvm](https://github.com/lvntky/cvm)** — A Java Virtual Machine implementation in C++.
- **[WarpFrame](https://github.com/lvntky/WarpFrame)** - Rendering engine for PlayStation 1-era graphics
- **[ccontainer](https://github.com/lvntky/ccontainer)** - Comprehensive STB style header-only library of data structures implemented in C

Here are a few projects in which I made even a small contribution, and which I am proud of:

- **[raysan5/raylib](https://github.com/raysan5/raylib)** - A simple and easy-to-use library to enjoy videogames programming
- **[epasveer/seer](epasveer/seer)** - Seer - a gui frontend to gdb
